🚀 MatuPay

WhatsApp Commerce Trust Layer for Kenya
📱 How MatuPay Works (User Journey)
1
Seller Posts Product
Sarah posts a blue dress on Instagram story: "New collection! DM to order"
Platform: Instagram/WhatsApp (seller's existing channels)
📸
↓
2
Buyer Sends Inquiry
Jane sees the dress and sends: "Nataka dress ya blue, size M"
Channel: WhatsApp DM to seller's business number
💬
↓
3
MatuPay Bot Intercepts
Instead of Sarah responding manually, MatuPay WhatsApp bot replies instantly:
"✅ Order Confirmation: Blue Dress (Size M) - 2,500 KES
Seller: Sarah's Boutique ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (127 sales)
Reply CONFIRM to pay via M-Pesa"
🤖
↓
4
Payment via M-Pesa
Jane replies "CONFIRM" → M-Pesa STK Push notification appears on her phone
She enters PIN → Payment of 2,500 KES sent to MatuPay escrow account
Key: Money is NOT sent directly to seller yet!
💳
↓
5
Escrow Hold + Notifications
Buyer (Jane) sees: "✅ Payment confirmed! Order #MP-156. Money held safely in escrow."
Seller (Sarah) sees: "🎉 New Order! #MP-156 - Blue Dress - 2,500 KES (in escrow). Package and deliver."
🔒
↓
6
Seller Ships Item
Sarah packages the blue dress (size M) and delivers via courier or hand delivery
She marks order as "Shipped" in her dashboard
📦
↓
7
Buyer Inspects & Confirms
Jane receives dress and checks: ✓ Correct color ✓ Correct size ✓ Good condition
She replies to WhatsApp bot: "RECEIVED"
If problem: She can reply "DISPUTE" and upload photos
👀
↓
8
Payment Released to Seller
MatuPay releases payment from escrow:
Total: 2,500 KES → Platform fee (2%): 50 KES → Seller receives: 2,450 KES
Money transferred to Sarah's M-Pesa instantly!
Auto-release: If Jane doesn't respond in 24 hours, payment auto-releases
💰
🏗️ System Components
📱 WhatsApp Bot
  • Receives buyer inquiries
  • Sends order confirmations
  • Triggers M-Pesa payments
  • Handles delivery confirmations
  • Manages disputes
💳 M-Pesa Integration
  • STK Push (payment requests)
  • Payment callbacks
  • B2C payouts to sellers
  • Refunds to buyers
  • Transaction tracking
🔒 Escrow System
  • Hold payments securely
  • 24-hour auto-release timer
  • Manual release on confirmation
  • Refund processing
  • Fee calculation (2%)
🖥️ Seller Dashboard (Web)
  • View all orders
  • Track payments
  • Mark orders as shipped
  • Manage disputes
  • View earnings & analytics
⚖️ Dispute Resolution
  • Photo evidence upload
  • Automated decision (AI)
  • Manual admin review
  • 6-hour evidence deadline
  • Partial refunds option
⚙️ Background Tasks
  • Auto-release scheduler
  • Dispute deadline checker
  • Daily seller reports
  • SMS notifications
  • Analytics aggregation
